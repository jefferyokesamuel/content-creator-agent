# Copywriting

You write copy that converts — hooks that stop the scroll, headlines that get clicks, CTAs that drive action, and body copy that holds attention.

## Copy Frameworks

### PAS — Problem-Agitate-Solution
1. **Problem** — Name the pain the audience feels
2. **Agitate** — Make it hurt more (why this problem matters)
3. **Solution** — Present your answer

### AIDA — Attention-Interest-Desire-Action
1. **Attention** — Hook that stops them
2. **Interest** — Keep them reading
3. **Desire** — Show why they want this
4. **Action** — Tell them what to do next

### 4Ps — Picture-Promise-Prove-Push
1. **Picture** — Paint their current situation
2. **Promise** — What their future looks like
3. **Prove** — Evidence, testimonials, data
4. **Push** — CTA that makes it easy to act

## Hook Patterns (Social Media & Video)

1. **Pattern Interrupt** — "Stop scrolling if you want [result]"
2. **Curiosity Gap** — "The #1 mistake people make with [topic]"
3. **Specific Number** — "I did [X] for [Y] days and here's what happened"
4. **Contrarian** — "Everyone says [X], but here's the truth"
5. **Relatable Admission** — "I used to think [wrong thing] too"
6. **Before/After** — "Here's where I was 6 months ago vs. now"
7. **Direct Challenge** — "If you think [X], prove it"
8. **Question** — "What would you do if [scenario]?"

## CTA Patterns

- **Direct:** "Comment [keyword] for the guide" / "Share this with someone who needs it"
- **Curiosity:** "You won't believe what happened next (link in bio)"
- **Urgency:** "This only works for the first [number] people"
- **Community:** "Follow for more [topic] tips"
- **Soft CTA:** "Save this for later" / "Tag someone who needs to hear this"

## Headline Scoring

Strong headlines are:
- **Specific** — numbers, names, concrete details
- **Urgent** — implies timeliness or scarcity
- **Useful** — reader knows what they'll get
- **Curiosity-driving** — gap between known and unknown

Weak headlines are:
- Generic ("Tips for success")
- Vague ("How to improve your life")
- Non-specific ("Things you should know")
