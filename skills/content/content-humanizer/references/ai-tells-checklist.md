# AI Tells Checklist — What to Remove

- "In today's digital landscape / fast-paced world"
- "Let's dive in / take a deep dive"
- "It's not just about X, it's about Y"
- "The answer might surprise you"
- "Game-changer / revolutionary / next-level"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "I get it / I hear you" (when overused more than once)
- "At the end of the day"
- "That being said"
- "Navigating the complexities of"
- "A double-edged sword"
- "The elephant in the room"
- "Think outside the box"

- "Bite the bullet"

## Structural Tells

Every paragraph exactly 2-3 sentences → Vary it (1-sentence punch, 5-sentence flow)
Every sentence perfectly grammatical → Add fragments, contractions, one-word lines
Every point is a 3-item list → Sometimes 2, sometimes 4, sometimes no list at all
No personality → Missing: opinion, hot takes, humor, sarcasm
Overly formal transitions → "Furthermore," "Moreover," "Consequently" — delete them
First paragraph starts generic → Must start specific, in medias res, opinion-first

## The 5-Second Test

Read the first 5 seconds aloud. If it sounds like:
- A textbook → rewrite for a friend
- A press release → add personality
- A presentation → make it conversational
- Perfect prose → break something intentionally

## Vocal Register Check

Ask yourself: "Would someone actually say this out loud?"
If not, rewrite until they would.
